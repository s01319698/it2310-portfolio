About Lab 2

In this lab, I am learning how absolute and relative file paths work and tables. I learned the differences and advantages of absolute and relative file paths. I also learned how to make a table in HTML. I then learn how to organize a table when it gets complicated by including thead, tbody, and tfoot elements. Finally, I learned how to make a table more accessible to people who have bad eyesight or are blind.
